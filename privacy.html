Google and YouTube data (internal upload tool)

Anderson Factory (Anderson Education LLC) runs an internal tool that uploads short videos to YouTube channels it owns and operates. It is not offered to the public, and only the channel owner signs in to it.

What it accesses: with the owner's permission through Google sign-in, it uses the YouTube Data API to read the signed-in channel's basic details (channel name and ID, and view counts of its own uploads) and to upload videos to that channel.

How it is used and stored: this data is used only to upload the owner's videos, check that each upload went to the right channel, and pause a channel if its uploads stop performing. Sign-in tokens and upload records are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Removal: access can be revoked at any time at Google account permissions; local tokens and records are deleted on request or when the tool is retired.

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
